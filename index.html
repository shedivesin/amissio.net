Amissio

‘ὦ Τίμαρχε, τί ποθεῖς πυθέσθαι;’
“Timarkhos, what do you want to know?”

‘πάντα, τί γὰρ οὐ θαυμάσιον;’
“Everything, since what here isn't bewildering?”

(Ploutarkhos on the Angel of Sokrates §22.)

Amissio is Latin for "loss;" in the divinatory art of geomancy, it symbolizes lacking, giving, and devotion. This website is the little gift of a philosopher and mystic, who is always seeking to understand the bewildering circumstances which we humans find ourselves in, to those who similarly seek a way Beyond.

Transcriptions

We moderns have access to a wealth of public domain texts on the Internet, but these are often in the form of poor-quality scans, which are not very pleasant to read. I have transcribed a number of these using LaTeX, so that I might print and read them more comfortably.

Geomancy

Geomancy is a divinatory art, and is useful as such, though I think it is even more interesting as a sort of calculus or logical system for understanding change. I have made several tools in order to facilitate its online discussion.
